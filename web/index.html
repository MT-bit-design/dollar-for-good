How much good does each dollar do? Sourced numbers, plain math, no guesses.

The idea behind this tool

Some giving does far more good per dollar than other giving. This tool helps you see where a donation goes furthest, using published evidence and showing where every number comes from.

Effective altruism is the movement that uses evidence to find where giving helps most. The Centre for Effective Altruism describes it as “a philosophy and movement that encourages people to combine compassion and care with evidence and reason to find the most effective ways to help others.” (centreforeffectivealtruism.org, accessed 2026-10-26)

A limit to keep in mind. This method favors causes that are easy to measure. Work like policy change or AI safety is hard to put a number on, so it is hard to rank here. Not being ranked here does not mean a cause matters less.

      The idea

      Some giving does far more good per dollar than other giving. Effective altruism is the movement that uses evidence to find where giving helps most. The Centre for Effective Altruism describes it as “a philosophy and movement that encourages people to combine compassion and care with evidence and reason to find the most effective ways to help others.” (centreforeffectivealtruism.org, accessed 2026-10-26)

      The honest limit. Measuring good per dollar favors causes that are easy to measure, like health or income. Work like policy change or AI safety is hard to put a number on, so it is hard to rank. Not being ranked here does not mean a cause matters less.

      Rules this tool follows

      • Every number has a source link, a date, and the exact quote it came from. A number that is not in its own quote is not used.
      • A number that can't be sourced is marked missing. Nothing is guessed.
      • All math is done by plain code. The AI model only finds and quotes figures; it never calculates.
      • Website text is treated as untrusted. Instructions hidden in pages are ignored, and a quote that isn't word for word on a page that was actually opened is rejected.
      • Each lookup has hard limits on model calls, pages opened and tokens, plus a daily token budget. Browsing the rankings never uses the AI model.
